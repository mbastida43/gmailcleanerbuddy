Privacy Policy

Effective date: June 17, 2026 · Last updated: July 23, 2026

Gmail Cleaner Buddy ("we," "our," or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how our application processes information when you use our tool to identify and clean the top 10 email senders causing clutter in your Google inbox. It applies to both the web app and the Android app (package com.mbastida.gmailcleanerbuddy).

1. Information We Collect and Process

Gmail Cleaner Buddy only accesses your Google account data via the official Gmail API after you grant explicit permission.

  • Scope of access: we request the https://www.googleapis.com/auth/gmail.modify scope, which allows reading email metadata and moving messages to Trash.
  • Data processed: the application reads only email metadata (sender addresses and volume/size counts) to identify the top senders ("offenders"). It never reads the body or subject of your messages.

2. How We Use Your Data

The data accessed is used exclusively for the core functionality of the application:

  • To count and display the top 10 senders filling up your inbox.
  • To move messages from those specific senders to Trash, only when you explicitly command it.

3. Data Storage and Retention

We do not store your data.

  • All processing happens in your active session or in temporary memory needed to execute the API calls.
  • No email content, sender addresses, or personal information is ever saved to an external database, server, or persistent storage managed by us.
  • On the Android app, the Google access token lives only in memory and expires in about one hour; nothing is written to the device.

4. Data Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell, trade, rent, or share any of your personal information or email data with third parties. Your data remains entirely within your Google ecosystem and your active local session.

5. Google API Services User Data Policy

Gmail Cleaner Buddy's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs to any other app will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

6. Security

We implement standard security measures to protect your active session. Since we do not host or store your data on external servers, the security of your emails relies on Google's infrastructure and your own device's security. Emails are moved to Gmail's Trash (recoverable for 30 days) and are never permanently deleted by the app.

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated effective date.

8. Contact Us

If you have any questions or concerns about this Privacy Policy, please contact the developer via the project repository: github.com/mbastida43/gmailcleanerbuddy.

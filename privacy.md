# Privacy Policy for Gmail Cleaner Buddy

**Effective Date:** June 17, 2026

Gmail Cleaner Buddy ("we," "our," or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how our application processes information when you use our tool to identify and clean the top 10 email senders causing clutter in your Google Inbox.

## 1. Information We Collect and Process
Gmail Cleaner Buddy only accesses your Google account data via the official Gmail API after you grant explicit permission. 
* **Scope of Access:** We request permission to read and manage your emails (e.g., `https://googleapis.com` or `https://googleapis.com`).
* **Data Processed:** The application analyzes email metadata (such as sender addresses and volume counts) strictly to identify the top 10 most frequent email senders ("offenders").

## 2. How We Use Your Data
The data accessed is used exclusively for the core functionality of the application:
* To count and display the top 10 senders filling up your inbox.
* To assist you in deleting or archiving messages from those specific senders, based on your direct command.

## 3. Data Storage and Retention
**We do not store your data.** 
* All processing happens locally in your session or in temporary memory required to execute the API calls.
* No email content, sender addresses, or personal information is ever saved to an external database, server, or persistent storage managed by us.
* Your Google OAuth tokens are handled securely and are not stored by the application beyond the active session.

## 4. Data Sharing and Disclosure
We do not sell, trade, rent, or share any of your personal information or email data with third parties. Your data remains entirely within your Google ecosystem and your active local session.

## 5. Google API Services User Data Policy
Gmail Cleaner Buddy's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs to any other app will adhere to the [Google API Services User Data Policy](https://google.com), including the Limited Use requirements.

## 6. Security
We implement standard security measures to protect your active session. Since we do not host or store your data on external servers, the security of your emails relies directly on Google's infrastructure and your own device's security.

## 7. Changes to This Privacy Policy
We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected directly in this file with an updated effective date.
